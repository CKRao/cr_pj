AI Ops bridges the gap between research models and production-grade systems. Focus on monitoring, model versioning, and safe rollouts.

Essential practices:

- Continuous evaluation against real-world benchmarks.
- Canary rollouts with feature flags for model switching.
- Input/output validation and rate-limiting for safety.
- Centralized logging and alerting for anomalies in agent behavior.
